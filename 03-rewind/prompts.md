# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Can we break down the data by the responders? Particularly the ones getting the most calls and those getting the least after 12 Aug. Break it down by week

### 2.

Can you visualize the data in a way that is easier to read? perhaps in a line graph

### 3.

These tell a good story. Let's find a single number that we can report out to leadership that speaks to the problem of some heroes not getting pings and maybe why

### 4.

Next step: what looks to have led those 4 to getting less and less pings over time?

### 5.

Compare the support_tickets table on rook-database against the pings table. Do they agree with each other? When did people start writing in, and when do the numbers actually move?

### 6.

Are there tickets for those at the end that are receiving more tickets post Aug 12?

### 7.

For the heroes that got lower numbers but don't have a lot of tickets referencing it, do they have a history of submitting tickets prior to the 4.2 release?

### 8.

Is there anything in the files you have access to that justify the change from 90 seconds to 60 seconds for responses?

### 9.

Pick one responder from the file who went quiet. Show me every week for them, how many times we pinged them, how many they took. Then tell me what happened to that person, week by week, in plain English.

### 10.

What were Undertow's response times to calls prior to the 12 Aug change?

### 11.

Is there anything from Undertow's tickets that suggest they were unavailable for a time?

### 12.

Let's do a week by week breakdown of Undertow's pings and responses

### 13.

Hmm, tell me more about the callouts that never get an accepted ping

### 14.

Why are some callouts only sending out 1 ping? Is there a pattern that when someone's ranking hits 0 they receive no more pings?

### 15.

How difficult is it for a responder who has fallen so low to get back in good standing?

### 16.

If I had only asked for the problem, what would I have missed?

### 17.

What feels like the best next steps to solve our 4.2 problems we've circled on?

### 18.

What could we be missing by me just asking for solutions? What should I be asking instead?
